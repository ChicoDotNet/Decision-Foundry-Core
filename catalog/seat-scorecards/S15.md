## Canonical stage contract

Council II sits on the **Manager → Director** transition and asks: **Does the synthesis faithfully represent the Specialist work?** Its stage-quality checks cover question coverage, omissions, duplication, conflicts, causality, priorities and synthesis integrity.

Each Elder evaluates only one canonical criterion:

- **Justice:** affected parties and obligations are treated fairly.
- **Mercy:** unnecessary harm is avoided while accountability is preserved.
- **Prudence:** timing, reversibility and foreseeable consequences are considered.
- **Stewardship:** money, time, authority and resources are used responsibly.

## Authority boundary

These scorecards assess criterion-specific Certification judgment, not production authorship. Each Elder:

- evaluates only the assigned material Manager-to-Director transition;
- issues one criterion-specific vote with rationale;
- preserves evidence, dissent, contradictions and invalidation conditions;
- may attach observations, conditions and escalation signals;
- does not rewrite Manager or Specialist work;
- does not claim another Elder criterion;
- does not emit the collective Council II status alone;
- does not approve the underlying business decision.
